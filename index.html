How an enquiry is handled

  1. You enquire. Pick a property or send a general question.
  2. We record it. You get a reference number only once it is safely saved.
  3. A person follows up. Availability and inspection times are confirmed by our team, never promised automatically.

How these numbers are counted
  • Completed sales/rentals use the verified completion date; submitted/acquired properties use the submission date; commission uses approval/payment dates.
  • A property submitted by several agents counts once as a company acquisition; credit follows the approved attribution.
  • A listing, reservation, enquiry or cancelled/disputed deal is never a completed sale. Commission calculated ≠ approved ≠ paid.

About HavenKey Abuja

HavenKey Abuja is a fictional demonstration brand. It shows how a real estate agency in Abuja can advertise properties, capture enquiries and agents, and keep management informed automatically.

The technology behind the demonstration is built by Agbaje Automation: website forms, an n8n workflow, an Airtable CRM, Google Gemini analysis and Gmail notifications.

This is not a registered company. No awards, testimonials, certifications or verified listings are claimed.

What the automation does

  • Saves every enquiry once, even if you press submit twice.
  • Summarises it with AI and sets a rule-based priority.
  • Creates a follow-up task for a person.
  • Emails an acknowledgement and tells the right managers instantly.
  • Sends a daily summary to management.
